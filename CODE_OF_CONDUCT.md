# Code of conduct

DashDeck adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming, be respectful, criticise ideas rather than people, and accept that
the maintainer makes the final call on what fits the project.

Harassment, personal attacks, and publishing anyone's private information are not
tolerated. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/jasonPenner83), and they will be handled confidentially.
Consequences range from a warning to a permanent ban, as described in the Covenant's
enforcement guidelines.
