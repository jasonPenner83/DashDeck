maplibre-gl 6.12.0, from https://registry.npmjs.org/maplibre-gl/-/maplibre-gl-6.12.0.tgz
published 2026-10-03, BSD-3-Clause, LICENSE-maplibre-gl.txt beside it.

Vendored rather than loaded from a CDN: the dash is launched by hand in a truck (C5) and
nothing between the process starting and a drawn map should need the network to resolve.
Taken a fortnight after release rather than at latest, so a fresh regression has had time
to surface before it reaches a vehicle.

ESM, and three files rather than one: maplibre-gl.mjs imports ./maplibre-gl-shared.mjs and
resolves ./maplibre-gl-worker.mjs against import.meta.url, so all three must sit together
and be served from a real origin. That is why the page is on a virtual host and not file://.
