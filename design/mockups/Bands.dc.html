THE RULES
Nav switches the region below the stage. Maps does not stop because you opened a card. The stage is a running layer with its own lifecycle. Tap a widget and it expands in place. History and detail open below the stage, not over it. The stage yields bands instead of being replaced. Six bands are a shared budget. Whatever is below asks for what it needs; the stage takes the remainder, down to a floor of one band. Settings is the exception. It takes all six and hides the stage, because it is used parked.
